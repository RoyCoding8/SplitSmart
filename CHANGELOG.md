# Changelog

## 1.0.0 (2026-09-25)

First release.

- Groups with per-group currency and per-group self identity
- Expenses with equal, exact, percent, and shares split modes, participant subsets, and live preview
- Simplified debt plan with raw/simplified toggle; partial and full settlements
- History search and category filter
- Recurring expense templates that generate due expenses automatically, with duplicate protection and bounded catch-up
- Per-group CSV export, JSON backup and restore, and lossless restore of group
  colour, custom categories, receipt photos, and the "you" identity per group
- Manual exchange rates for foreign-currency settlements
- Membership lifecycle: remove-member guard, archive and delete groups, archived tab
- Home-screen balance widget and app shortcuts
- Bill and settle-up reminders delivered by a background worker
- Material 3 dynamic theme and dark mode
- Receipt photos survive a backup and restore round trip, including on paths that need URI escaping
